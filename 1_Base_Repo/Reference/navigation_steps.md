1 Log in to the EzDocs application and navigate to the “Received Property” LOB.
2 Create a new submission under Received Property, or use an existing submission.
3 Click on “Show CIIM.” This will navigate to the IPS Admin screen, where you can enter the required data in the appropriate tabs based on the logic.
4 After entering all the required data, click on “Save.” You will be redirected back to the EzDocs screen.
5 On the EzDocs screen, click on “Sync CIIM.” This ensures that the data entered on the IPS screen is synced and populated correctly in EzDocs.
